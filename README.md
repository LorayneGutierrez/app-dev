# The Parent Trap (1998)

> A story about two girls who swap places to bring their divorced parents back together.

---

## Overview

**The Parent Trap** is a *family comedy* directed by **Nancy Meyers**. It is a remake of the 1961 Disney film, which was based on the 1949 German novel *Das doppelte Lottchen* by Erich Kästner.

## Main Characters

1. **Hallie Parker** – raised in California by her dad
2. **Annie James** – raised in London by her mom
3. **Nick Parker** – Hallie's father, a winemaker
4. **Elizabeth James** – Annie's mother, a wedding gown designer
5. **Meredith Blake** – Nick's fiancée
